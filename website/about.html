About Yellow Cabs Whitecourt

A locally owned taxi service built on reliability, safety, and genuine care for the community we drive every day.

Whitecourt, ABProudly local

Rooted in Whitecourt, Built for Our Community

Yellow Cabs Whitecourt is a locally owned and operated taxi service serving Whitecourt and the surrounding area. We know these streets because we live here — and that local knowledge means shorter waits, smarter routes, and drivers who genuinely care about getting you where you're going.

Our tagline, "Your Journey, Our Priority," isn't just a slogan on the side of our cars. It's how we run the business: courteous drivers, dependable pickups, and a fleet that's kept clean and road-ready every single day.
